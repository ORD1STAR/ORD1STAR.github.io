Master’s final-year project

A brain switch,
stress-tested.

Detecting when someone moves from rest to a command, from 30-channel EEG, for a person the model has never seen. The interesting part was not the model. It was proving the number meant something. Scroll to follow the work and the numbers it produced.

  1. 01
    Record

    Ten participants, two cue protocols, a 30-channel headset.

    10 participants · 28 recordings · 30 ch · 500 Hz

  2. 02
    Window

    Causal filters with carried state. Nothing sees the future.

    2 s windows · 0.5 s hop · causal

  3. 03
    Describe

    Covariance matrices, causal Riemannian re-centering, tangent-space features.

    covariance · tangent space · log-power

  4. 04
    Decode

    Four decoders, scored on people the model has never seen.

    Riemann 0.671 · ShallowConvNet 0.666 · EEGNet 0.631 · ensemble 0.707 AUC

  5. 05
    Doubt it

    Drift-matched nulls, a time-only control and an independent code review.

    null 0.489 · timestamp-only 0.811 vs EEG 0.514 · legacy audit 98% → 36%

  6. 06
    Replay live

    A streaming decoder over Lab Streaming Layer, replaying a held-out recording.

    17–19 ms per decision · every onset detected

  7. 07
    Verdict

    Reported as it came out, including what did not work.

    3-class 0.385 vs 0.333 chance · brain switch about 0.60–0.64 after review · push vs walk not decodable
